01 / SCIENTIFIC FOCUS

Research areasAraştırma alanları

01

Brain–Computer InterfacesBeyin–Bilgisayar Arayüzleri

In our laboratory at Ege University, we are working hard to bridge the gap between human cognition and assisted technologies. At this point, our research focuses on decoding neural signals to develop innovative methods for cutting-edge BCI applications and innovative human-technology interaction systems. By combining our expertise in neuroscience, signal processing, and artificial intelligence, our mission is to put forward novel neural interfaces that provide mobility, enhance cognitive capabilities, and push the boundaries of future human-computer interaction.Bu araştırma alanının Türkçe açıklaması hazırlanıyor.

02

Brain Dynamics & ConnectivityBeyin Dinamikleri ve Bağlantısallık

In our studies, we delve into the temporal and spatial networks of the human brain. Our research focuses on capturing the functional and effective brain connectivity by analyzing complex neural dynamics and tracking how neural information flows during cognitive tasks and pathological states. By integrating electrophysiological recordings, neuroimaging techniques, and computational methods, we aim to uncover the latent mechanisms of brain network organization for developing new neurotechnological, clinical diagnostics, and cognitive enhancement (rehabilitation) devices.Bu araştırma alanının Türkçe açıklaması hazırlanıyor.

03

Neurograph Signal ProcessingNörograf Sinyal İşleme

We investigate brain signals by graph system approximations. Our research focuses on developing new algorithms for understanding of brain complex dynamics via analysing of brain graph-based signals or data. For this aim, we use vertex and spectral based mathematical methods. We also integrate AI techniques and significant graph-based features to decode cognitive attributes of brainBeyin sinyallerini, sinir sistemlerinin çizge tabanlı yaklaşımları aracılığıyla inceliyoruz. Araştırmalarımız, çizge tabanlı nöral sinyal ve verileri analiz ederek beynin karmaşık dinamiklerini anlamaya yönelik yeni algoritmalar geliştirmeye odaklanmaktadır. Bu amaçla düğüm (vertex) ve spektral tabanlı matematiksel yöntemlerden yararlanıyoruz. Ayrıca beynin bilişsel özelliklerini çözümlemek için yapay zekâ tekniklerini anlamlı çizge tabanlı özniteliklerle birleştiriyoruz.

04

NeurobiophotonicsNörobiyofotonik

We also operate at the intersection of optics, photonics, and neuroscience to visualize and probe the living brain with light. Our research focuses on developing non-invasive optical imaging, advanced fluorescence microscopy, and biophotonic signal processing techniques to monitor neurovascular dynamics and neural activity in real time. By harnessing light-tissue interactions, we aim to design new diagnostic methods, track brain function at micro- and macro-scales, and develop light-based therapeutic and neuro-monitoring solutions for clinical applications.Bu araştırma alanının Türkçe açıklaması hazırlanıyor.

05

Modelling & Simulation of Neural SystemsSinir Sistemlerinin Modellenmesi ve Simülasyonu

At Ege University, we combine computational neuroscience, mathematical modeling, and computer simulations to unravel how neural circuits process the incoming information from the complex environment. Our research focuses on developing biophysically realistic neuron models, simulating complex brain networks, and mapping multiscale neural dynamics from cellular channels to whole-brain systems. By bridging theoretical frameworks with empirical neural data, we aim to uncover fundamentals of neural computation, elucidate neurological disease mechanisms, and inform the design of novel neurotechnologies.Bu araştırma alanının Türkçe açıklaması hazırlanıyor.

06

AI for Neural System AnalysisSinir Sistemi Analizinde Yapay Zekâ

We integrate artificial intelligence (AI)-based tools into our studies to unravel complex brain dynamics. Our research leverages machine learning algorithms, deep neural networks, and advanced pattern recognition to process large-scale electrophysiological and neuroimaging data. By developing intelligent predictive models and automated diagnostic pipelines, we aim to uncover neural mechanisms, discover clinical biomarkers, and develop next-generation brain-inspired technologies.Bu araştırma alanının Türkçe açıklaması hazırlanıyor.

07

NeurocardiologyNörokardiyoloji

We explore the intricate bidirectional communication between the central nervous system and the heart. Our research for this theme mainly focuses on decoding autonomic nervous system control, analyzing heart rate variability (HRV), and mapping neuro-cardiac interactions during cognitive processing, emotional states, and cardiovascular disorders. By integrating advanced electrophysiological recordings (EEG/ECG), signal processing, and computational modeling, we aim to discover novel autonomic biomarkers, advance non-invasive cardiac-brain monitoring, and pave the way for innovative diagnostic and therapeutic strategies.Bu araştırma alanının Türkçe açıklaması hazırlanıyor.
